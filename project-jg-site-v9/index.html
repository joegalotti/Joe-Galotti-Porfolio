My Background

From journalism to a marketing utility man

My career started with journalism. I earned my journalism degree from Stony Brook University with aspirations of becoming a sports writer, and storytelling has remained at the center of my work ever since.

After college, I began managing social media for a local recreational sports facility. That experience helped me make the jump into content marketing in 2017, when I joined Answering Legal as a content writer.

What began with blog writing quickly expanded. Over more than nine years at Answering Legal, I took on responsibilities across social media, email marketing, podcasting, live events, public relations, customer campaigns, strategic partnerships and more. In 2021, I was promoted to Head of Public Relations.

Because I worked within a lean marketing team, I became what I like to call a “marketing utility man”: someone comfortable jumping between disciplines, learning new tools and taking ownership wherever the team needed help.

My journalism background never really disappeared. The research, interviewing, writing and storytelling skills I developed early in my career became the foundation for everything that followed.

After nearly a decade helping Answering Legal build its content and communications programs, I’m now looking for the next place where I can put that range of experience to work.
