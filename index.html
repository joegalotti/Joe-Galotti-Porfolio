Dear Hiring Manager,

I’m excited to apply for a content and communications role where I can bring the range of skills I’ve developed during more than nine years at Answering Legal.

As Head of Public Relations, I move constantly between strategy and execution. On a given day I may be directing a podcast edit, writing a newsletter for thousands of recipients, coordinating a customer testimonial project, building PR around a new product or developing a proposal for a state bar association.

What distinguishes my experience is how hands-on that work remains. I don’t simply oversee content from a distance. I write, produce, publish, coordinate, review, analyze and improve it.

This portfolio provides a closer look at how that work happens.
